# RSK-001 — Ransomware halts warehouse operations

RSK-001 Ransomware halts warehouse operations: current risk 'Low', ALE EUR 485k (90% CI EUR 105k–EUR 1.22M), within appetite.

## What is the estimated risk?

The current expected annual loss (ALE) is EUR 485k, with a 90% credible interval of EUR 105k to EUR 1.22M. The probability of at least one loss event in a year is 16%. In a 1-in-20 bad year, losses reach EUR 3.23M or more (VaR 95%). The average of those worst 5% of years is EUR 6.27M (expected shortfall).

## Why is it at this level?

0.179 expected loss events/yr → likelihood 'Low'; EUR 2,666,705 expected loss per event → impact 'High'. The risk matrix gives 'Low'. The ALE of EUR 485k is compared with the scenario threshold of EUR 500k, and the level with the maximum acceptable level 'Moderate'. The risk is **within appetite**. Given the parameter uncertainty, the probability that the true ALE is within the threshold is 67%. The analyst's qualitative rating (likelihood 3 × impact 4 → 'Moderate') is DIFFERENT from the quantitative result.

## How confident are we?

The ALE credible interval spans a factor of 11.6. 5 of 18 inputs are data-derived; the rest are expert judgement or assumptions. The largest single source of uncertainty in the ALE is Threat event frequency (rank correlation +0.84), so better measurement of this input would narrow the estimate most. 3 of the 5 controls credited today are tested effective. The Monte Carlo error on the ALE is ±EUR 12k (20,000 trials). This is simulation precision only and does not reflect input uncertainty.

## Which controls reduce it?

The linked controls, as implemented and tested, reduce the ALE from EUR 4.07M (inherent) to EUR 485k, a reduction of 88%. The most critical controls are CTL-IAM-01 (+EUR 359k if it failed), CTL-EDR-01 (+EUR 317k if it failed), CTL-EML-01 (+EUR 289k if it failed).

## What if the assumptions change?

The main drivers, each moved from its P10 to its P90, are Threat event frequency (EUR 174k–EUR 881k), Susceptibility (EUR 294k–EUR 672k), Loss magnitude: operations_halted (EUR 321k–EUR 697k). Stress tests: TEF ×2: ALE EUR 966k (+99%), level 'Moderate'; Magnitude ×2: ALE EUR 970k (+100%), level 'Moderate'; CTL-EML-01 fails: ALE EUR 774k (+60%), level 'Moderate'; CTL-IAM-01 fails: ALE EUR 844k (+74%), level 'Moderate'; CTL-EDR-01 fails: ALE EUR 802k (+65%), level 'Moderate'; CTL-VUL-01 fails: ALE EUR 587k (+21%), level 'Moderate'; CTL-BKP-01 fails: ALE EUR 675k (+39%), level 'Low'.

## What mitigation has the greatest effect?

The largest risk reduction comes from option T3 'Cyber insurance': ALE −EUR 334k (±EUR 7k, paired estimate), VaR95 −EUR 2.54M. Its annualised cost is EUR 95k (ROSI +251%), and the residual level would be 'Low'. Other options: T1 ALE −EUR 170k, cost EUR 193k/yr; T2 ALE −EUR 74k, cost EUR 113k/yr. The selected treatment gives a target ALE of EUR 315k ('Low').

## What assumptions were made?

- Loss events follow a Poisson process given the annual rate (events are independent in time).
- Controls acting on the same factor combine multiplicatively (independent layers of defence).
- A control's operating rate is the probability it works for a given event; failures are independent between events.
- Loss forms of the same event are positively correlated through a one-factor Gaussian copula.
- Loss-magnitude ranges describe event-to-event variability; uncertainty about severity parameters is not modelled separately (v1).
- Inherent risk is the risk without the controls linked to the scenario; the wider environment is unchanged.
- Quantitative banding: likelihood from expected loss events per year, impact from expected loss per event.
- Business-interruption cost per day comes from the 2026 BIA; seasonality is not modelled.
- Ransom payment is excluded because board policy is not to pay.
